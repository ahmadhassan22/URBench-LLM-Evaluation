### New finding: five false "present" matches

- **Cause:** `norm()` lowercases titles.
  - D2 counts a gold title as present if any corpus title matches after lowercasing
    (`d2_title_coverage.py:96,115`).
  - D3 and D4 then fetch chunks by the same normalized match (`d3_oracle_retrieval.py:59,105-106`, used by
    `d4_extract_facts.py:47-49`).
- **Effect:** when the real article is missing but an all-caps acronym article exists, the acronym article is
  used.
