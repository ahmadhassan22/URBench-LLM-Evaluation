## 3. Issues found in the pipeline

### Method issues (not fixed)

- **I1. Stage 0, Urdu entity to English title, is the D6 bottleneck (pre-window).**
  - Proof: 82/143 = 57.3% of generated titles exist in the corpus, and 42/200 = 21.0% overlap the gold
    titles (`logs/d6_endtoend_FULL_62905.log:85,89`; `outputs/efbpt/d6/d6_summary.json`).
  - Recorded cause: transliteration drift (والکری → "Walcott"), the wrong entity ("goats" → "Sheep") and
    non-canonical forms ("Mustache") (`experiments.md:4570-4585`).
  - Not fixed:
    - D7 fuzzy matching was rejected on arithmetic. Verified: 15 rows have E1 wrong and X1 right; 5 of them
      have no matched title. Rescuing all 5 gives 71.83%, p=0.0931, and b≥18 would be needed for p<0.05.
    - L0 constrained linking failed its gate (`outputs/efbpt/l0/l0_summary.json`): LINKABLE B 45.64%, R@10
      11.85%, delivered 10.07% against a 51.64% pass threshold.
- **I2. Structural ceiling.** StrategyQA gold titles are often justification pages; 52.5% of gold
  title-instances have no lexical trace in the question, and this is an upper bound
  (`experiments.md:4605-4617`, `README.md:448`). Unverified: I found no file that produces this number.
- **I3. Corpus defect.** 70.47% of gold titles are present and 71/200 questions are fully covered
  (`experiments.md:4112-4133`). Not recomputed this session; not fixed (a different corpus is required).
- **I4. N25 retrieval does not reach the child targets.**
  - Recall@10 (`score_v2/scores.json`, verified): A 12%, P 12%, C 20%, B 28%, D 28%, E 38%.
  - 16 of 36 targets appear in their own question's retrieved candidates
    (`reachability.json`, `positive_controls`); 20 never do (T:51fef34d@13:53:08).
  - Infrastructure checks pass:
    - 36/36 titles exist in the metadata (`reachability.json` summary);
    - 1,046/1,046 target vectors align;
    - 15,000 scores reproduce, with a maximum difference of 2.5e-7 (`target_vector_probe_v1/summary.json`
      t2, t3).
  - A target's own title retrieves it in the top 10 for 25/36 targets. Seven are not even in the raw top
    100: frog, jacinda ardern, dromedary, padmé amidala, potamon fluviatile, marten, animal
    (`title_self_retrieval.jsonl`, verified).
  - Cause: not identified. The allowed readings are weak dense-encoder title matching, competition from
    other vectors, duplicate titles or a weak document vector (`summary.json` t5 `interpretation_limit`).
- **I5. The query writer loses the question's meaning.**
  - Examples: Sable/Marten, Hedgehog and Bactrian camel (`docs/EFBPT_BRIDGE_V2_DEV_NOTE.md` §1). The first
    record of `parent_v2/queries.jsonl` is "Audio watermarking Audi A8 L Plus".

    `rubric_review_completed.jsonl` joined with `review_key/worksheet_key.jsonl`).
  - Cause: the frozen prompt had no meaning-preservation rule (T:8239bd25@05:41:33).
  - Not fixed: the V2 prompt moved R@10 for D from 28% to 30% and for B from 28% to 24%.
  - These are AI judgments, not native-speaker judgments.
